I am Yulong Cao, a research scientist in NVIDIA's [Autonomous Systems and Physical AI Research (ASPIRE) Group](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/aspire/), led by [Marco Pavone](https://research.nvidia.com/person/marco-pavone).
My research interests include trustworthy AI, alignment, and embodied AI systems. My research goal is to build secure and safe autonomous systems aligned with human values.
I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at the [University of Michigan](https://www.eecs.umich.edu/cse/), advised by [Morley Mao](http://web.eecs.umich.edu/~zmao/). Before that, I received B.S. degrees from both the [University of Michigan](https://www.eecs.umich.edu/cse/) and [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](http://www.sjtu.edu.cn/). My [Ph.D. work](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/handle/2027.42/176634) examined vulnerabilities in autonomous driving systems and ways to make them more robust.
